# Lightsaber Customization Quick Reference

## How to Build a Lightsaber

### Step 1: Collect Components
You need at least one of each:
- **Kyber Crystal** - Determines base power and alignment
- **Hilt** - Affects grip, balance, and defense
- **Emitter** - Controls blade projection and stability

### Step 2: Press 'B' to Open Builder
The lightsaber builder will guide you through:

1. **Choose Blade Type**
   - Single Blade: Balanced (no modifiers)
   - Double-Bladed: +5 Attack, -2 Defense (aggressive)
   - Crossguard: +3 Attack, +3 Defense (versatile)
   - Dual Wield: +4 Attack, +2 Evasion (agile)

2. **Select Your Crystal**
   - View stats and descriptions
   - Crystal quality affects damage

3. **Select Your Hilt**
   - Different hilts provide bonuses
   - Some enable special styles

4. **Select Your Emitter**
   - Affects accuracy and blade stability
   - Higher quality = better stats

5. **Preview Assembly**
   - See visual diagram
   - Check combat stats
   - View your blade color
   - Confirm or cancel

## Blade Colors & Corruption

Your lightsaber blade color reflects your alignment:

### Light Side Path (Low Corruption)
- **Crystal Blue (0-9)**: Pure Light, selfless Jedi
- **Azure Blue (10-19)**: Traditional Jedi Guardian
- **Verdant Green (20-29)**: Light Side protector
- **Emerald Green (30-39)**: Jedi Sentinel

### Gray Jedi Path (Balanced)
- **Violet Purple (40-49)**: Questioning, seeking balance
- **Gold Yellow (50-59)**: True neutrality, Temple Guard

### Dark Side Path (High Corruption)
- **Amber Orange (60-69)**: Dark Side influence
- **Dark Orange (70-79)**: Corruption spreading
- **Blood Red (80-89)**: Dark Side warrior
- **Crimson Red (90-100)**: Sith Lord

**Your blade color updates automatically** when your corruption changes!

## Learning Lightsaber Forms

### How to Learn Forms
Press **M** to meditate, then select **"Perform Ritual"**.

Choose a Form Meditation ritual:

### Form I: Shii-Cho (Basic)
- **Requirements**: None (all Jedi start with this)
- **Corruption**: 0-100 (universal)
- **Level**: 1+
- **Benefits**: Fundamental bladework

### Form III: Soresu (Defensive)
- **Requirements**: Light Side alignment
- **Corruption**: 0-40
- **Level**: 5+
- **Benefits**: +5 Defense, deflection mastery

### Form V: Shien (Offensive)
- **Requirements**: Balanced alignment
- **Corruption**: 30-70
- **Level**: 8+
- **Benefits**: +5 Attack, aggressive counters

### Form VII: Juyo (Fury)
- **Requirements**: Dark Side alignment
- **Corruption**: 60-100
- **Level**: 12+
- **Benefits**: +8 Attack, +5 Corruption (WARNING!)

### Changing Forms
1. Learn forms through meditation rituals
2. Press **X** to switch between learned forms
3. Each form changes your combat style

## Blade Type Strategies

### Single Blade (Standard)
- **Best For**: Beginners, balanced playstyle
- **Combat**: Reliable, no penalties
- **Forms**: Works with all forms

### Double-Bladed (Aggressive)
- **Best For**: Offensive builds, crowd control
- **Combat**: High damage, lower defense
- **Forms**: Great with Shien (V) or Juyo (VII)

### Crossguard (Versatile)
- **Best For**: Hybrid builds, dueling
- **Combat**: Balanced offense/defense boost
- **Forms**: Excellent with Soresu (III)

### Dual Wield (Agile)
- **Best For**: High-skill players, evasion builds
- **Combat**: Fast attacks, dodge focus
- **Forms**: Pairs well with any form

## Tips & Tricks

### Color Management
- **Track your corruption** with the character sheet (C key)
- **Light Side actions**: Purifying artifacts, helping NPCs, peaceful solutions
- **Dark Side actions**: Using forbidden powers, aggressive choices, keeping dark artifacts
- **See color shift messages** when your blade changes

### Form Synergy
- **Soresu + Crossguard** = Maximum defense tank build
- **Juyo + Double-Blade** = Berserker damage dealer
- **Shien + Dual Wield** = Balanced aggressive build
- **Form I + Any** = Solid foundation for any style

### Component Quality
- **Rare crystals** = Higher base damage
- **Legendary hilts** = Best stat bonuses
- **Unique emitters** = Special effects
- **Mix and match** for your ideal build

### Ritual Planning
- **Check requirements** before attempting meditation
- **Save form rituals** for appropriate corruption levels
- **Plan your path**: Light → Soresu, Dark → Juyo
- **Stance Change ritual** lets you adapt mid-game

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I rebuild my lightsaber?**  
A: Yes! Press B anytime to reconstruct with different parts or blade type.

**Q: Does blade color affect stats?**  
A: No, color is purely cosmetic and reflects your alignment. Stats come from components.

**Q: Can I learn all forms?**  
A: No, some forms require specific corruption ranges. Choose your path wisely.

**Q: What happens if I change alignment after learning a form?**  
A: You keep learned forms, but can't learn new ones outside your corruption range.

**Q: Can I use two different colored blades with Dual Wield?**  
A: Currently no, but this may be added in future updates.

**Q: Does blade type affect Force powers?**  
A: No, blade type only affects melee combat stats.

**Q: Can I find special blade types?**  
A: All blade types are available in the builder. Find better components for stronger sabers.

**Q: What's the best blade type?**  
A: It depends on your playstyle:
  - Defensive: Crossguard
  - Aggressive: Double-Bladed
  - Balanced: Single Blade
  - Evasive: Dual Wield

## Lore Notes

### Blade Colors in Canon
- **Blue**: Jedi Guardians (combat focused)
- **Green**: Jedi Consulars (Force focused)
- **Purple**: Balance of both sides (Mace Windu)
- **Yellow**: Jedi Sentinels, Temple Guards
- **Orange**: Rare, often Gray Jedi
- **Red**: Sith bleed crystals through dark side corruption

### Form Origins
- **Form I (Shii-Cho)**: First form, taught to all younglings
- **Form III (Soresu)**: Developed after blasters became common
- **Form V (Shien/Djem So)**: Offshoot of Form III, more aggressive
- **Form VII (Juyo/Vaapad)**: Most demanding, channels aggression

### Blade Types in Star Wars
- **Single**: Most common, Cal Kestis, Luke Skywalker
- **Double-Bladed**: Darth Maul, Bastila Shan (KOTOR)
- **Crossguard**: Kylo Ren (unstable crystal vents)
- **Dual Wield**: Ahsoka Tano, Asajj Ventress
