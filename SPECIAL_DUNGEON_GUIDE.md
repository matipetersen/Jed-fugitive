# Special Dungeon Complete Guide

## Overview
Special dungeons are multi-level thematic areas with unique enemies, rich lore, and powerful artifacts. Each dungeon has 3-5 levels that must be descended before reaching the artifact chamber.

---

## How to Enter & Exit

### **Entering**
- Find special dungeon entrances marked with unique symbols on the surface map:
  - `M` - Memory Vault
  - `G` - Crystal Garden  
  - `S` - Shadowmaze
  - `B` - Bone Cathedral
  - `E` - Echo Chambers
  - `L` - Twisted Library
  - `F` - Pain Forge
  - `D` - Dream Nexus

### **Navigating Levels**
- Each dungeon has multiple floors (3-5 levels)
- Use **down stairs `>`** to descend deeper
- Each level has unique lore and progressively tougher enemies
- The **artifact `◆`** appears only on the final level

### **Exiting**
1. Find the artifact `◆` on the final level
2. Make your choice: **Absorb**, **Destroy**, or **Leave it**
3. After interacting, **exit stairs `<`** appear where the artifact was
4. Step on the exit stairs to return to where you entered

## Enemy Difficulty Scaling

**Base Threat Level**: Enemies scale with:
1. Your character level
2. Current dungeon floor (+1 level per floor)
3. More enemies spawn on deeper floors (2-4 on floor 1, up to 6-9 on final floor)

**Unique Enemy Stats**:
- **Memory Wraith**: Moderate HP, high evasion, confusing attacks
- **Crystal Guardian**: Very high HP, high defense, low evasion
- **Shadow Stalker**: Low HP, very high evasion, high attack
- **Bone Wraith**: High HP, moderate stats, undead resilience
- **Echo Banshee**: Moderate HP, sonic damage attacks
- **Knowledge Seeker**: Moderate HP, magical attacks
- **Pain Wraith**: High HP, pain-inflicting strikes
- **Nightmare Herald**: High HP, fear-based attacks

## Strategic Tips

1. **Explore carefully** - Each floor has unique lore and atmosphere
2. **Manage resources** - Healing items are valuable in multi-floor dungeons
3. **Enemy scaling** - Later floors have significantly tougher enemies
4. **Artifact decision** - Absorbing grants power but increases corruption
5. **Exit strategy** - You can only exit after interacting with the artifact
6. **No backtracking** - Once you descend, you must reach the artifact to escape

---

## Corruption Impact

Your choice at the artifact determines your moral path:

- **Absorb**: Gain powerful abilities, increase corruption (Dark Side)
- **Destroy**: Gain defensive/peaceful abilities, decrease corruption (Light Side)  
- **Leave**: No effect, but you're stuck until you make a choice

Higher corruption affects:
- NPC interactions
- Available dialogue choices
- Travel log narrative tone
- Force ability effectiveness
- Ending scenarios

---

## Finding Special Dungeons

- **Surface World**: 1-3 special dungeons spawn randomly on the surface
- **Marked Entrances**: Look for the unique symbol letters (M, G, S, B, E, L, F, D)
- **One-Time Exploration**: Each dungeon can only be completed once per playthrough
- **Optional Content**: Not required for main story but provide powerful rewards

## Example Playthrough

```
1. Find entrance "S" (Shadowmaze) on surface
2. Enter → See "Penumbral Entrance" lore popup
3. Fight Shadow Stalkers (▪)
4. Find down stairs (>), descend to level 2
5. See "Twilight Corridors" lore popup
6. Fight more Shadow Stalkers (stronger)
7. Descend through levels 3, 4, eventually reach level 5
8. See "The Void Mirror Chamber" lore
9. Find artifact ◆ (Mirror of the Void)
10. Choose: Absorb (+28 corruption) or Destroy (-18 corruption)
11. Exit stairs < appear where artifact was
12. Climb stairs to return to surface world
```
